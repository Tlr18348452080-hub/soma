## 1. 催产素（oxytocin）与社会信任

### 效应有多大

- `[A]` **面孔情绪识别 g = 0.29**（7 项研究，381 人，其中女性 71 人）
  > "Results showed that intranasal OT administration enhances emotion recognition of faces overall, with a Hedges g effect size of 0.29."
  > —— PMID 23575742（2013, Neuropsychopharmacology）

- `[S]` **临床组总效应 d = 0.32**（19 项临床试验，N=304，95%CI 0.18–0.47，P<0.01）；分病种只有孤独症谱系显著，**d = 0.57**（N=68，95%CI 0.15–0.99，P<0.01）
  > "The combined effect size was d=0.32 (N=304; 95% confidence interval (CI): 0.18-0.47; P<0.01). However, of all disorders, only studies on autism spectrum disorder showed a significant combined effect size (d=0.57; N=68; 95% CI: 0.15-0.99; P<0.01)."
  > —— PMID 23695233（2013, Translational Psychiatry）

- 内群体/外群体信任（13/8/10 个效应量，N=408/317/505）：摘要只说了「内群体信任升高、外群体信任未被显著降低」，**没给效应量** → 数字未找到。
  > "We found that intranasal oxytocin administration enhances the recognition of facial expressions of emotions, and that it elevates the level of in-group trust. The hypothesis that out-group trust is significantly decreased in the oxytocin condition was not supported."
  > —— PMID 21802859（2012, Psychoneuroendocrinology）

### 反面证据（必须一起读）

- `[S]` **大样本复现没做出来。** 重复 Kosfeld 2005，n=211；与 Declerck 2020（n=321）合并后 n=532，等效检验判定效应「小到不值得做实验室研究」。
  > "We found no evidence that intranasal OXT administration increases trusting behavior, neither in our data nor in the pooled dataset. While equivalence testing in our dataset was inconclusive, equivalence testing in the pooled dataset (n = 532) indicated that the effect of OXT administration on trusting behavior lies within a minimal range of effects, suggesting that the effect is too small to be of interest to (most) lab-based studies due to feasibility constraints of collecting data from larger samples required to reject smaller effect sizes."
  > —— PMID 41880977（2026, Cortex）

- `[S]` **发表偏倚有实据。** 某实验室 2009–2014 的 8 项研究（13 个因变量、25 种范式、453 名被试），结果「太常常不是预期的那样」。
  > "For eight studies (including 13 dependent variables overall, as assessed through 25 different paradigms) performed in our laboratory between 2009 and 2014 on 453 subjects, the results obtained were too often not those that were expected."
  > —— PMID 26991328（2016, J Neuroendocrinol）

- `[S]` 威胁加工 26 项研究（N=1173）：只提高健康人的**生理惊跳反应**（small effect size），注意偏向、注视、趋避行为都不显著。
  > "A single dose of intranasal oxytocin significantly increased the physiological startle response to threat in healthy people with a small effect size. However, oxytocin did not have significant effects on attentional bias towards social or disorder-specific threat, fixation towards threatening stimuli among healthy or clinical populations, or on threat related behavioural approach or avoidance responses."
  > —— PMID 28837950（2018, J Affect Disord）

- `[G]` 综述结论：外周催产素测量方法本身有问题，基因关联在大样本里不复现。
  > "We conclude that the cumulative evidence does not provide robust convergent evidence that human trust is reliably associated with OT (or caused by it)."
  > —— PMID 26581735（2015, Perspect Psychol Sci）

**怎么用**：可以写「方向有，但效应量小到 0.2–0.3 量级，且大样本复现失败」。不要写「催产素让人更信任别人」这种定性句。

## 2. 内啡肽 / 阿片系统

### 跑步后 β-内啡肽变化量

- `[A]` **升 1.4–1.9 倍，不是几倍几十倍。** 14 名女性马拉松跑者：马拉松前基线 22 pmol/L、跑台前 19 pmol/L；马拉松后 30 min 升 **1.4 倍**，递增跑台后升 **1.9 倍**。
  > "Baseline concentrations for beta-endorphin of 22 pmol.l-1 before the marathon and 19 pmol.l-1 before the treadmill exercise increased 1.4-fold 30 min after the marathon and 1.9-fold after the treadmill exercise"
  > —— PMID 8925811（1996, Eur J Appl Physiol）

- `[A]` **中枢证据是受体占据，不是血浓度。** 10 名运动员，静息 vs 2 小时耐力跑（21.5 ± 4.7 km）做 [18F]FDPN PET；跑后阿片受体可用性在额前与边缘/旁边缘区下降，欣快感评分与额前/眶额、前扣带、双侧岛叶的阿片结合**负相关**。
  > "Ten athletes were scanned at 2 separate occasions in random order, at rest and after 2 h of endurance running (21.5 +/- 4.7 km). ... The level of euphoria was significantly increased after running and was inversely correlated with opioid binding in prefrontal/orbitofrontal cortices, the anterior cingulate cortex, bilateral insula, parainsular cortex, and temporoparietal regions."
  > —— PMID 18296435（2008, Cerebral Cortex）

> ⚠️ 上面两个数字不是同一件事：8925811 量的是**外周血浆浓度**，18296435 量的是**中枢受体占据**。外周 β-内啡肽能否过血脑屏障本身有争议，不能拿 1.4 倍直接说成「脑子里内啡肽涨了 1.4 倍」。

### 纳洛酮阻断后痛阈怎么变

- `[A]` **部分逆转，不是全逆转。** 12 名长跑者，6.3 英里 @ 85% 最大有氧能力；跑后静脉给纳洛酮 0.8 mg × 2（间隔 20 min）。跑后在生理盐水条件下出现痛觉迟钝（缺血痛报告显著下降、热刺激辨别力 P(A) 显著下降），纳洛酮**逆转了缺血性痛觉迟钝，没逆转热痛觉迟钝**；且纳洛酮削弱了跑后的愉悦、欣快等 VAS 评分。
  > "Sensory decision theory analysis of the responses to thermal stimulation showed that discriminability, P(A), was significantly reduced post-run under the saline condition, a hypoalgesic effect; response bias, B, was unaffected. Ischemic pain reports were significantly reduced post-run on the saline day, also a hypoalgesic effect. Naloxone reversed the post-run ischemic but not thermal hypoalgesic effects. Joy, euphoria, cooperation, and conscientiousness VAS ratings were elevated post-run; naloxone attenuated the e..."
  > —— PMID 6330643（1984, Pain）

- `[G]` **反方：阿片系统可能不是运动镇痛的主系统。** 58 人随机双盲交叉，纳曲酮 vs 安慰剂，两种条件下**都**出现运动镇痛。
  > "As reductions in pain (i.e., EIH) were observed following both conditions, these results suggest that the opioid system may not be the primary system involved in exercise-induced hypoalgesia and that 2-AG and 2-OG could contribute to nonopioid exercise-induced hypoalgesia."
  > —— PMID 28387833（2018, Pain Medicine）

- 内源性大麻素在跑后升高：60 分钟户外跑，AEA 与 2-AG 全体显著升高，但摘要里的 p 值被串成 "p p p p p < 0.05"，**具体数值不可引用**，只记方向。PMID 39330709（2024, Sports）
  > "Mood, AEA, and 2-AG concentrations were significantly increased post-run considering all participants (p p p p p < 0.05)."

## 3. 神经肽 Y（NPY）

### 有量化的两条

- `[A]` **PTSD 患者脑脊液 NPY 低于对照。** 10 名慢性战斗相关 PTSD 男性 vs 13 名健康男性，晨间 CSF：对照 **360.0 ± 17.7 pg/mL**，PTSD **233.6 ± 28.7 pg/mL**（p=.0008；校正年龄与 BMI 后 p=.003）。
  > "As compared with the normal comparison subjects, PTSD patients had significantly lower concentrations of CSF neuropeptide Y (mean CSF NPY was 360.0 +/- 17.7 pg/mL in control subjects but only 233.6 +/- 28.7 pg/mL in PTSD patients [p = .0008])."
  > —— PMID 19576571（2009, Biological Psychiatry）

- `[A]` **唾液 NPY 在高应激下没显著升高。** 15 名男性警用枪械教官，高应激射击任务前 1 h 唾液 NPY **1.56 ng/mL（SD 0.88）**，低应激训练日 **1.13 ng/mL（SD 0.52）**，差异不显著。
  > "Mean NPY levels (ng/mL) were higher before the high-stress shooting task (M = 1.56, SD = 0.88) than on the low-stress training day (M = 1.13, SD = 0.52), but this difference did not reach significance."
  > —— PMID 42148315（2026, Front Hum Neurosci）

### 只有方向、没有数字的

- `[S]` 35 项研究的 meta：PTSD 患者**血浆与 CSF** NPY 均低于对照；MDD 患者**仅血浆**低、CSF 不低；慢性应激人群血浆 NPY **高于**对照。药物与性别可解释 27% 的组间方差。摘要**未给 SMD 具体值** → 数值未找到。
  > "NPY levels were significantly lower in plasma and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) in PTSD patients versus controls. Patients with MDD had significantly lower levels of NPY in plasma compared to controls, but not in the CSF. ... However, chronic stress patients had significantly higher plasma NPY levels compared to controls, PTSD or MDD."
  > —— PMID 32048334（2020, J Neurosci Res）

- `[A]` 美军生存训练：49 人训练前后 + 21 人在战俘审讯后采血，审讯后 NPY 显著升高，特种部队高于非特种部队。**摘要无具体浓度** → 数值未找到。PMID 10807963（2000, Biol Psychiatry）

- `[G]` 大鼠单次鼻喷 NPY 可减轻 SPS 模型的抑郁样与焦虑样行为、减弱 ACTH/皮质酮升幅。**动物，不能直接外推人**。PMID 23376740（2013, Neuroscience）

**因果方向警告**：CSF NPY 低是 PTSD 的**伴随特征**，可能是长期应激的结果，也可能本身是易感因素——19576571 自己写的是 "possibly related to the disorder or to extreme stress exposure per se"，横断面数据分不出先后。

## 4. 血清素与情绪（色氨酸耗竭 ATD）

### 效应量

- `[S]` **健康人身上基本没有。** 45 项 ATD 研究可合并：5-HT 耗竭**没有**降低健康对照的情绪；只有 MDD 家族史的健康人「轻微」降低；缓解期 MDD 患者出现**中等**情绪下降。摘要**未给具体效应量数值** → 数值未找到。
  > "5-HT or NE/DA depletion did not decrease mood in healthy controls. 5-HT or NE/DA depletion slightly lowered mood in healthy controls with a family history of MDD. In drug-free patients with MDD in remission, a moderate mood decrease was found for ATD"
  > —— PMID 17389902（2007, Molecular Psychiatry）

- `[S]` 焦虑方面 21 项研究：ATD 在任何组都**没有**一致地改变焦虑；ATD 后的挑战试验也没能稳定诱发焦虑（只有 35% CO2 挑战在现患惊恐障碍中稳定升高焦虑）。
  > "ATD did not consistently affect anxiety in any of the groups. Moreover, a challenge test after ATD (n = 17 studies) did not consistently provoke anxiety in healthy volunteers or remitted patients."
  > —— PMID 33574223（2021, Translational Psychiatry）

### 耗竭有多深（这是唯一拿得到的「阈值」类数字）

- `[A]` **游离色氨酸/LNAA 比降 93%**（安慰剂日降 1%）。8 名 SSRI 缓解期 OCD 患者，交叉设计，耗竭饮后 5 h 测。同时 VAS 显示「控制感下降、侵入性思维增加」。
  > "Free plasma tryptophan to large neutral amino acid ratio decreased by 93% on the depletion day and decreased by 1% on the sham day, as anticipated. Psychological rating scores as measured by Visual Analogue Scale showed a significant decrease in perceived control and increase in int..."
  > —— PMID 29095069（2017, J Psychopharmacol）

- `[A]` 28 名健康男性：ATD 确实降低了血浆色氨酸，但**重评（reappraisal）能力、额叶活动、HRV 全都没变**。
  > "Results indicated that ATD reduced plasma tryptophan, and reappraisal was effective in modulating emotional experience in the emotion regulation task. However, ATD had no significant effect on reappraisal ability, frontal activity, and HRV."
  > —— PMID 37430145（2023, Cogn Affect Behav Neurosci）

> **「最低色氨酸浓度阈值」：未找到。** 检索到的文献给的是「相对基线下降百分比」（93%），没有一篇给出「低于 X µmol/L 才出现情绪下降」的绝对阈值。别把 93% 写成阈值——它是操作剂量，不是阈值。

## 5. 睾酮与攻击 / 竞争

### 赢家效应有多强

- `[S]` **总体很小，出实验室才明显。** 60 个效应量、>2500 名被试：赢家睾酮升幅大于输家，**D = 0.20**，异质性很高。分场景：实验室外（运动场等）**D = 0.43**；实验室内**D = 0.08**，且只在男性中成立（男 **D = 0.15**，女 **D = −0.04**）。
  > "Results from a meta-analysis involving 60 effect sizes and >2500 participants indicated that winners of a competition demonstrated a larger increase in testosterone concentrations relative to losers (D=0.20)-an effect that was highly heterogeneous. This 'winner-loser' effect was most robust in studies conducted outside the lab (e.g., in sport venues) (D=0.43); for studies conducted in the lab, the effect of competition outcome on testosterone reactivity patterns was relatively weak (D=0.08), and only found in studies of men (D=0.15; in women: D=-0.04)."
  > —— PMID 27720891（2017, Hormones and Behavior）

### 与支配行为

- `[S]` **双激素假说只是勉强成立。** 30 篇 / 33 项研究 / 49 个效应量 / n=8538：睾酮 × 皮质醇 对地位相关行为（地位、支配、冒险、攻击、精神病态）的交互效应 **r = −.061（p = .026）**，非常小；稳健性分析提示发表偏倚、分析自由度、单项研究功效不足。
  > "There was only marginal support for the dual-hormone hypothesis: The effect size of the interaction between testosterone and cortisol on status-relevant behavior was significant but very small (r = -.061, p = .026)"
  > —— PMID 30529754（2019, Neurosci Biobehav Rev）

- `[A]` 连续两日对抗任务：第一日与胜负一致的睾酮反应可预测第二日任务表现；两场都输的人睾酮下降最陡。**摘要无具体数值** → 数值未找到。PMID 25001950（2014, Psychoneuroendocrinology）

**因果方向**：D=0.20 是「赢了之后睾酮升得更多」的**反应性**效应，不是「睾酮高所以会赢」。反过来用就是循环论证。

## 6. 甲状腺激素

### 甲减 / 亚临床甲减 与抑郁

- `[S]` 21 项研究入综述，7 项合并（共 103,375 人）：亚临床甲减者抑郁风险 **OR = 1.78（95%CI 1.11–2.86，P = 0.02）**；Egger t = −0.49（P = 0.647）、Begg z = −0.15（P = 0.881），未见发表偏倚。
  > "The meta-analysis showed that persons with SCH had a significantly elevated risk of depression than persons with euthyroidism (OR = 1.78, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 1.11-2.86, P = 0.02). No publication bias was found, as indicated by Egger's test (t = -0.49, P = 0.647) and Begg's test (z = -0.15, P = 0.881)."
  > —— PMID 31214119（2019, Front Endocrinol）

- `[S]` 12,315 人合并：亚临床甲减者抑郁风险 **RR = 2.35（95%CI 1.84–3.02）**。
  > "In a pooled analysis of 12,315 individuals, those with SCH had higher risk of depression than euthyroid controls (relative risk 2.35, 95% confidence intervals [CI], 1.84 to 3.02; p"
  > —— PMID 30621645（2019, BMC Psychiatry）

- `[A]` 纵向队列 6191 人（平均 50.2 岁，67% 女性）：TSH ≤0.3 或 >4.2 mIU/L 者，临床相关抑郁（PHQ-9 ≥10）风险 **HR = 1.33**。
  > "Among 6191 adults (mean age, 50.2 years, 67% female, 92.4% White), thyroid dysfunction was significantly associated with an increased CRD risk (hazard ratio [HR], 1.33; P"
  > —— PMID 42400584（2026, Mayo Clin Proc）

### 反面证据：补甲状腺素不改善情绪

- `[S]` **RCT，n=245 妊娠期亚临床甲减，左甲状腺素 vs 安慰剂：抑郁评分无差异。** CES-D 中位数 **10（5–15） vs 10（5–17），P = .46**。
  > "Treatment with thyroxine was not associated with differences in CES-D scores (10 [5-15] vs 10 [5-17]; P=.46) or in odds of screening positive in the third trime..."
  > —— PMID 32168208（2020, Obstet Gynecol）

> **「甲减时抑郁的发生率」：未找到具体百分比。** 检索到的 meta 给的是 OR / RR（相对风险），不是发生率。30621645 的摘要提到做了「SCH 中抑郁患病率」的亚组，但摘要在给出数值前被截断——不能拿 OR 反推成发生率。

## 7. 皮质醇与情绪

### 横断面相关有多小

- `[S]` **瞬时情绪与唾液皮质醇：r ≈ .05–.06。** 22 项 EMA 研究；负性情绪 k=19，38,418 个瞬时观测，**r = .06（p < .001）**；正性情绪 k=15，31,721 个瞬时观测，**r = −.05（p = .003）**。无方法学调节变量。
  > "Meta-analysis found a significant positive association between momentary negative emotions and cortisol (r = .06, p < .001) and a significant negative association between momentary positive emotions and cortisol (r = -.05, p = .003). No methodological differences moderated these associations."
  > —— PMID 33662445（2021, Neurosci Biobehav Rev）

### 抑郁 vs 对照

- `[S]` 20 项病例对照（1354 例抑郁 / 1052 对照）：晨唾液皮质醇高 **2.58 nmol/L（95%CI 0.95–4.21，p = 0.002）**，晚高 **0.27 nmol/L（95%CI 0.03–0.51，p = 0.03）**。但参考区间高度重叠（抑郁 0–29 nmol/L，对照 1–23 nmol/L），**没有区分能力**。
  > "In a random-effects meta-analysis salivary cortisol was increased for depressed patients as compared to control persons on average 2.58 nmol/l (95% C.I.: 0.95-4.21) p=0.002 in the morning and on average 0.27 nmol/l (95% C.I.: 0.03-0.51) p=0.03 in the evening. ... The reference intervals for morning salivary cortisol in depressed patients (0-29 nmol/l) and control persons (1-23 nmol/l) showed substantial overlap suggesting lack of discriminative capacity."
  > —— PMID 20447770（2010, Psychoneuroendocrinology）

### 因果检验（孟德尔随机化）

- `[S]` **皮质醇对焦虑有一点因果迹象，对抑郁没有。** 用 SERPINA6/SERPINA1 变异做工具变量：焦虑 **OR 1.16/每 z 分（95%CI 1.04–1.31）**；重性抑郁 **OR 1.02（0.95–1.10）**；神经质 **β −0.025（95%CI −0.071 到 0.022）**。
  > "Cortisol was associated with anxiety (pooled odds ratio [OR] 1.16 per cortisol z score; 95% CI, 1.04 to 1.31), but not major depression (pooled OR 1.02, 95% CI, 0.95 to 1.10) or neuroticism (β -0.025; 95% CI, -0.071 to 0.022)."
  > —— PMID 38525495（2024, Biol Psychiatry Glob Open Sci）

### 急性升高对效价的方向

- `[A]` **静脉氢化可的松不提升情绪，反而轻微降低「唤醒」。** 4 名健康志愿者，0/25/50/100/200 mg 五次日，血皮质醇大幅升高但无任何兴奋样主观效应。
  > "Despite large increases in circulating levels of cortisol, hydrocortisone did not produce any detectable stimulant-like effect on mood or vital signs. To the contrary, hydrocortisone had a mild sedative-like effect, decreasing 'arousal'."
  > —— PMID 11710752（2001, Behav Pharmacol）

> **「急性皮质醇→效价」的 meta 分析：未找到。** 检索到的 meta（33662445）合并的是**瞬时情绪与皮质醇的相关**，不是给药实验；给药实验（11710752）只有 n=4，是 preliminary study，只能记方向、不能记幅度。**方向记录：arousal 下降，valence 无提升。**

> ⚠️ **相关 ≠ 因果（这一节最要紧）**：20447770 是横断面病例对照，「抑郁者皮质醇高 2.58 nmol/L」说的是**组间差**，不是「皮质醇高导致抑郁」。而 38525495 用遗传工具变量做的因果检验里，皮质醇对 MDD 的 OR 是 1.02、CI 跨 1——**因果链在抑郁上没通过检验**。把前者当机制用是错的。

## 8. 情绪二维模型（valence × arousal）与测量

### 模型本身

- `[G]` 环形模型（circumplex）：所有情绪状态来自两个**独立**神经生理系统的认知解释产物——这是「二维」的理论依据，也是对基本情绪理论的替代方案。**叙述性综述，无量化。**
  > "The circumplex model of affect proposes that all affective states arise from cognitive interpretations of core neural sensations that are the product of two independent neurophysiological systems."
  > —— PMID 16262989（2005, Dev Psychopathol）

### 测量工具

- `[A]` **SAM（Self-Assessment Manikin）**：非言语图形量表，测 pleasure / arousal / dominance 三维，只需 **3 个判断**（对照 Mehrabian & Russell 语义差异量表的 **18 个评分**）。摘要写「两种方法在 pleasure 与 felt arousal 上相关很高」，但**未给相关系数** → 具体 r 值未找到。
  > "The Self-Assessment Manikin (SAM) is a non-verbal pictorial assessment technique that directly measures the pleasure, arousal, and dominance associated with a person's affective reaction to a wide variety of stimuli. ... which requires 18 different ratings. ... Correlations across the two rating methods were high both for reports of experienced pleasure and felt arousal."
  > —— PMID 7962581（1994, J Behav Ther Exp Psychiatry）

- `[A]` **Affect Grid**：单条目九宫格，测 pleasure–displeasure 与 arousal–sleepiness 两维。284 名大学生，与 BDI、PANAS、POMS 对照：**「中等效度」**，但对具体情绪性质**缺乏区分力**。
  > "In this study, scores on the Affect Grid were obtained from 284 college students and correlated with scores on the Beck Depression Inventory, Positive and Negative Affect Schedule, and the Profile of Mood States. Factor analytic and correlational findings suggest that the Affect Grid is a moderately valid measure of the general dimensions of pleasure and arousal but has little specificity in discriminating among various qualities of affective experience."
  > —— PMID 9819938（1998, Psychol Rep）

> **「把激素映射到 valence × arousal 两个轴」的原始文献：未找到。** 检索到的都是「情绪 → 二维」的模型与量表（16262989 / 7962581 / 9819938），没有一篇把具体激素浓度作为自变量、把 valence/arousal 作为因变量并给出效应量的研究。这一层要自己做的话，缺的是**桥接文献**，不是量表。

## 附：哪些是「相关」不是「因果」

| 说法 | 实际证据类型 | 能不能当机制用 |
|---|---|---|
| 抑郁者皮质醇更高（+2.58 nmol/L） | 横断面病例对照（20447770） | **不能**。MR 检验里皮质醇→MDD 的 OR=1.02、CI 跨 1（38525495） |
| 皮质醇「导致」焦虑 | 孟德尔随机化，OR 1.16（38525495） | 可以说「有因果迹象」，但效应小，且是遗传工具变量的局部平均效应 |
| PTSD 患者 CSF NPY 低 | 横断面 10 vs 13（19576571） | **不能**。原文自己写 "possibly related to the disorder or to extreme stress exposure per se" |
| 瞬时负性情绪与皮质醇相关 r=.06 | EMA 相关（33662445） | **不能**。r=.06 小到几乎没有解释力 |
| 赢家睾酮升得多（D=0.20） | 反应性效应的 meta（27720891） | 可以说「赢了之后升」，不能说「睾酮高所以赢」 |
| 亚临床甲减者抑郁风险 OR 1.78 / RR 2.35 | 横断面/病例对照 meta（31214119、30621645） | 只能说「风险关联」；补甲状腺素的 RCT 没改善情绪（32168208） |
| 鼻喷催产素提升信任 | 早期小样本 + meta（21802859、23695233） | **谨慎**。n=532 合并等效检验判为「小到不值得研究」（41880977） |
| 跑步后 β-内啡肽升 1.4–1.9 倍 | 单项 14 人实验（8925811） | 只能说外周血变化；中枢证据是 PET 受体占据（18296435），不是同一个量 |
